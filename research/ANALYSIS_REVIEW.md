## Company-specific thesis to test

Samsung must be valued as several different businesses: **DS memory** has HBM / server DRAM duration plus cyclical DRAM/NAND; **Foundry & S.LSI** depends on node yield, customer loading and capital intensity; **DX** depends on product mix and component cost; **SDC** is display-cycle and utilization-sensitive; **Harman** has auto/audio order and margin dynamics. AI exposure alone does not justify a premium: incremental profit must survive yield loss, depreciation and reinvestment, then convert to free cash flow.

## Peer rule

Use SK hynix / Micron for memory economics, TSMC for foundry operating benchmarks, display peers for OLED-cycle context, and device brands only as demand references for DX. Do not use one conglomerate PER to value all earnings pools. Negative or near-zero EPS is N/M in PER; value turnaround segments through explicit scenario cash flows or operating multiples with a disclosed recovery path.

## Release gate

After confirmed 3Q results, reconcile segment EBIT to consolidated EBIT; refresh net cash and equity attribution; rebuild the cash bridge; and rerun product-mix sensitivities before quoting a single central value.
